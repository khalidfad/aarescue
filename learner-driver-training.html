Questions

Can I also apply for a driver's licence?

Yes. This form covers driving lessons, a driver's licence, or both. Choose your service in step 1 and the form shows only what applies to you.

What happens after I submit the form?

Our team reviews your registration and contacts you to confirm your introduction session and lesson schedule.

Can I start if I am under 18?

Yes. Under-18 learners may begin learning to drive. Your details are recorded, and you are contacted to come for your licence when you turn 18.

How long do I have to finish?

Training and certification must be completed within three months of enrolment, and each certificate is valid for six months.

How do I join the Accelerated programme?

Accelerated enquiries are handled by direct contact with our team. Call 0802 555 1111 to get started.

    Conduct. AA Rescue instructors follow a professional code of conduct: courteous, tidy, punctual and ethical, with a clean, well-maintained car.

    Qualifications. AA Rescue is licensed by the Federal Road Safety Commission (FRSC) to give driving tuition.

    Eyesight and health. You must be able to read a number plate at 20.5 metres, with glasses if normally worn. Arrive fit to drive; the instructor may cancel the lesson if your judgement seems impaired by alcohol or drugs. Tell your instructor about any medication that may affect your driving.

    Payments. Payment is made in full at booking or at the start of the lesson and is valid for three (3) months. Where studies are deferred due to pregnancy and the company is notified, payment lasts six (6) months.

    Postponement. Amend or postpone at least 12 hours before the start time. AA Rescue may postpone lessons in unsuitable or dangerous weather or road conditions and is not responsible for costs arising from postponement.

    Damage. The vehicle is insured under road traffic law, but tyres and wheels are not covered. You are liable for damage during your lesson, especially if you do not follow your instructor.

    Punctuality. Record appointments in your Appointment Record Card. If you are more than 5 minutes late the lesson may be abandoned. Time lost through instructor delay is made up later.

    Comfort and safety. Wear suitable footwear and clothing that does not restrict movement. No indecent dressing.

    Training programme. All training must be completed within 3 months of the start of classes. AA Rescue advises on the best vehicle type (manual or automatic) and may insist on a particular type after assessing your needs.

    For office use: the driving school name, branch code and DS-ENR registration number, with the form tag and serial number, are assigned by our team after you submit.
