WHAT YOU GET

What is included

Breakdown recovery

Engine, gearbox or electrical failure on the road. We tow or lift your vehicle to a safe place.

Accident recovery

Safe removal of damaged vehicles from the scene.

Roadside help

Flat tyre, flat battery, fuel or lock-out support where possible.

Onward delivery

We take the vehicle to your mechanic, home or workshop.

FAQ

Common questions

Which cities do you cover?

Rescue is available 24 hours in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. For other locations, call us and we will advise.

Is the service available at night and on holidays?

Yes. Our team works every day of the year, including major holidays.

Can you move many vehicles at once?

Yes, see our multi-loader haulage service for intercity movement of several vehicles.
