WHO WE ARE

A pioneer in rescue and driver training

AA Rescue began with prompt emergency roadside assistance for stranded motorists. We are a pioneer of modern driving training and automobile rescue in Nigeria, and we have grown into a company that serves individuals, families and corporate organisations.
