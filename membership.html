QUESTIONS

Good to know

What counts as a member?

A member of AA Rescue is someone who has registered one or more vehicles with us. Your membership card also gives you discounts at selected business outlets.

Which vehicles do the plans cover?

Regular plans are priced by the number of MUVs and SUVs. Bullet-proof or armoured cars are charged at ₦90,000 per recovery in Lagos metropolis.

Do I have to be a member?

No. Non-members can use our one-off recovery, charged by distance. Specialised vehicles are charged based on the state of the vehicle, the location and the distance to the point of delivery.

Which areas are covered?

The 24-hour recovery service covers Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. Classic Membership B covers recoveries within Lagos State metropolis.

Which branch should I choose?

Choose the office nearest to you from the branches listed on this page. Each branch has its own code.

What reference do I use when I pay?

Use the reference on your invoice, for example AAR-RSC-00126. It starts with AAR, then a three-letter service code, then the job number and the year. You can pay on the Make a payment page.

How do I sign up?

Create an account with your name, username, email, phone number and password, choose a plan and you land in your dashboard.

Can I speak to someone first?

Yes. Call 0802 555 1111 (Lagos), 0802 555 3333 (Abuja) or 0802 555 6666 (Port Harcourt), or email customercare@aarescuenigeria.com.
