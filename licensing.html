          QUESTIONS

          Before you apply

          What documents do I need?

          It depends on the service. The form asks for the documents it needs, such as a valid ID, a passport photograph and, for renewals, your existing licence or papers. The licensing team confirms anything else when they contact you.

          Do I pay when I submit the form?

          No. Submitting the form is a request. The licensing team confirms the fees with you before they start.

          How long does it take?

          Timelines depend on the service and the issuing authority. The licensing team gives you an estimate when they contact you.

          Can I apply through WhatsApp instead?

          Yes. You can message the licensing team on WhatsApp and they will guide you through the process.

          Is my ID safe?

          We only ask for documents needed for your application. Send clear photos or scans, and call us on 0802 555 1111 if you have any concerns.

          Prefer to talk to someone?

          Our team is on the phone 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including major holidays.
