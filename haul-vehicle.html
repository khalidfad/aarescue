            QUESTIONS

            Before you book

            Why do you need my ID?

            We verify who is booking the haulage and who collects the vehicle, so cars only go to the right person.

            Which files can I upload?

            JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 5 MB each. Use clear photos with all corners of the document visible.

            What if the vehicle cannot move?

            Choose "Faulty (not moving)" as the condition and tell us where it is parked. We plan the right equipment for it.

            Can I send more than one vehicle?

            Yes. Add each vehicle in step 2 with its own papers and photos. Our multi-loader rigs carry six to ten vehicles per trip.

            How is the price decided?

            You can suggest a price in step 3. We review the route, vehicle types and quantity, then confirm a final quote before pickup.
