Terms and conditions for clients (one-off package)

  1. Processing takes 14 working days.
  2. You pay a service charge of 10% of the driver's annual gross salary before the request is processed. It applies to one driver; each additional driver is treated as a separate request with its own charge.
  3. AA Rescue deploys tested and trained drivers as requested.
  4. AA Rescue recruits on your behalf, so you are responsible for managing and caring for the driver, including paying salary. Paying statutory obligations (tax, pension), health care, training and uniforms is also advised.
  5. Confirm acceptance of the deployed driver to the Deployment Unit, Outsourcing Department, within 3 days of receiving the driver: email outsourcing@aarescuenigeria.com or SMS 08150846120.
  6. The service charge is not refundable once a driver has worked with you for more than 3 days.
  7. The service charge lapses after 12 continuous weeks. If you need another driver after that, a renewal charge of 8% of annual gross salary applies.
  8. No replacement is available after a driver and two replacements have been posted within 12 weeks of the first posting (4 weeks for a truck driver).
  9. If you do not wish to continue with a deployed driver at any stage, you pay the driver for the days worked.
  10. Treat drivers with due regard: no assault, insult, abuse or de-humanizing acts by you, your agents or your family. Drivers have signed a Code of Conduct and Ethics.
  11. Take up a comprehensive insurance policy and install a tracker on the vehicle or vehicles.
  12. You may not get a replacement if a driver alleges any act in clause 11 and, after investigation, AA Rescue reasonably establishes it to be true.
  13. During the contract, AA Rescue's liability for any claim by you is limited to twice the service charge paid.
