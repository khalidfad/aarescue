HOW IT WORKS

Help in three moves

Scroll to follow a rescue from the first call to the safe delivery of your vehicle.

  1. 1

    Call or share your location

    Ring our dispatch line or send your WhatsApp live location. We log the incident the moment you reach us.

  2. 2

    Rapid unit dispatch

    The closest rescue rig is sent from our nearest base, and you are kept updated until it arrives.

  3. 3

    Safe recovery & delivery

    Certified technicians secure your vehicle on a flatbed or fix it roadside, then deliver it to your home, office or workshop.
