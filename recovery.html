Vehicular Recovery and Rescue

A 24-hour service within Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. We recover broken-down vehicles from the point of incident or accident to wherever you need them.

Request a rescueCall 0802 555 1111

We come to you, then take your vehicle where it needs to go

Our recovery service moves a broken-down vehicle from the point of incident or accident to the location you choose, which could be your home or a car workshop.

We also recommend good auto mechanics to help with repairs, at the client’s cost. You can use the service as a member or as a one-off client.

What you get

24-hour service

Available round the clock within Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt.

Home or workshop

We take your vehicle to your house or the car workshop you choose.

Member or one-off

Register your vehicles as a member, or pay per rescue as a one-off client.

How it works

  1. Tell us where you areFill in the form or call us. Share your location so our team can find you.
  2. We dispatch helpOur team confirms the details with you and sends a recovery unit.
  3. We recover your vehicleYour vehicle is taken from the point of incident to your home or workshop.

Questions

Do I have to be a member?

No. You can use the service as a one-off client, billed per rescue. Members get a set number of recoveries a year plus other benefits.

Which areas do you cover?

The 24-hour recovery service covers Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt.

What happens after I submit the form?

Our team reviews your request and contacts you by phone to confirm details and next steps.

Is submitting the form a final commitment?

No. We confirm the details and any cost with you first, so you can check everything before we go ahead.

Can I speak to someone instead?

Yes. Call 0802 555 1111 at any time. Our line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
