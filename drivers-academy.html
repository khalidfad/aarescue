Questions

How long is my payment valid?

Payment is valid for six months. If your studies are deferred because of pregnancy or ill health and you tell us, it lasts 12 months.

Are Academy fees refundable?

No. Driving Academy fees are non-refundable, and all training must be completed within 3 months of starting classes.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear suitable footwear and professional clothing that does not restrict movement. You must be able to read a number plate at 20.5 metres, with glasses if you normally wear them. Tell your instructor about any medication that may affect your driving.

What happens after I submit the form?

Our team reviews your request and contacts you by phone to confirm details and next steps.

Is submitting the form a final commitment?

No. We confirm the details and any cost with you first, so you can check everything before we go ahead.

Can I speak to someone instead?

Yes. Call 0802 555 1111 at any time. Our line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Terms and conditions for driving lessons provided by AA Rescue Drivers Training Institute

These terms are the basis on which driving lessons and courses are offered to you.

  1. General conduct. AA Rescue instructors are bound by a professional code of conduct. They will be courteous, polite, tidy and punctual, and behave professionally and ethically at all times. Your instructor's car or truck will be well maintained, clean and tidy.
  2. Eyesight and health. You must be able to read a number plate at 20.5 metres, with glasses if normally worn. Present yourself for lessons in a fit state to drive, mindful of the laws on alcohol and drugs. The instructor may cancel the lesson if you seem unwell or your judgement seems impaired.
  3. Payments. Payment is made to AA Rescue on the agreed terms at registration or at the start of the lesson. It is valid for six (6) months, within which you must complete the classes. If your studies are deferred due to pregnancy or ill health and the company is notified, payment lasts 12 months.
  4. Postponement. AA Rescue may postpone lessons if weather or road conditions seem unsuitable or dangerous, and cannot be held responsible for any costs resulting from postponement, for whatever reason.
  5. Damage to the vehicle. The vehicle is insured under road traffic law, but damage to tyres and wheels is not covered. You are liable for any damage caused during your lesson, especially where you habitually do not follow your instructor's directions.
  6. Punctuality. Be punctual and record all appointments in your Log in Card to avoid mistakes. You may need to wait if the instructor is delayed by unforeseen circumstances. Lost lesson time is made up at the end of the lesson if possible, or at a later date.
  7. Learner comfort and safety. Wear suitable footwear and professional clothing that does not restrict movement; no indecent dressing. Tell your instructor before any lesson about medication that may affect your ability to drive safely.
  8. Training programme. All training must be completed within 3 months at the maximum from the start of classes. Driving Academy fees are non-refundable. Students must listen to and abide by their instructors' instructions, be of good character, and be respectful to instructors and other road users while training.
